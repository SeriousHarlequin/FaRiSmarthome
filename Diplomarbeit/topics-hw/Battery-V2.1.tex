\subsection{Hardware - V2.1}
    \textbf{There is no version 2.0 of the battery module. During the design process, 
        complications arose that directly led to the designation V2.1.}
    Version 2.1 of the battery module is identical to version 2.2, except for the
    following components:

    \begin{itemize}
        \item 1. A resistor was added to the boost converter circuit.
        \item 2. Some footprints were different in size.
    \end{itemize}

    \subsubsection{Boost Converter}
        A resistor was added to the boost converter to prevent it from turning off when the
        battery module is connected to the base module. The boost converter was not able to turn on, 
        when the battery module was connected to the base module. The boost converter turned off, 
        even though the battery USB-C was not connected. The reason for this was, that the  
        transistor was switched on duo to a small voltage that was present on the base of the
        transistor when it was connected to the base module. 

        \begin{figure}[H]
            \centering
            \includegraphics[width=0.4\textwidth]{assets/HW/BatteryV2.1-Boost-Converter.png}
            \caption{Boost-Converter implemented in version 2.1.}
        \end{figure}

        To fix this issue, a 47k\(\Omega\) resistor was added to the base of the transistor pulling 
        it to ground.
